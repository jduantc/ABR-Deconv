What exactly is being trained?

A 544,768-parameter residual MLP learns to predict v from a clean synthetic ABR after diffusion noise is added. It is trained on 20,000 filtered synthetic ABRs, with batches of 256, AdamW at learning rate 0.0002, a 500-update initial warmup, and no weight decay. The run continues the same optimizer trajectory started in the earlier 50,000-update experiment. Two exponential moving averages (EMA rates 0.001 and 0.005) follow the same weights; they are not independent training runs.

During reconstruction the weights are frozen. Starting from noise, the model repeatedly estimates a clean ABR and guidance adjusts the next waveform according to how well the estimate explains the EEG.

Three different quantities

Training v-MSE: error between predicted v and the v target for artificially noised clean ABRs. It measures denoising training performance, in scaled model units.

EEG residual RMS: difference between observed EEG and EEG predicted from the current estimated ABR. Its gradient guides reconstruction. The long run evaluation divides it by the known simulated noise standard deviation; this differs from your updated standalone reconstruction and the later guidance sweep.

ABR reconstruction RMSE: difference between the final reconstructed ABR and the known clean synthetic ABR, in µV. This is the quantity we want to minimize. It requires ground truth, which generally is unavailable for real EEG.

What we can conclude—and what remains unknown

  • The one-million-update model reconstructs the tested synthetic EEGs, and additional training to two million reduced validation RMSE under this protocol.
  • The EMA choices are close at long durations. The two-million versus five-million difference is small and comes from one training seed and a modest synthetic validation set.
  • The five-million checkpoint has a validation score, not the same independent interim test as the one-million checkpoint. The final test is planned at ten million and has not been completed.
  • Guidance 6.25 is promising for a different, unnormalized reconstruction protocol and the one-million checkpoint. It cannot be directly transferred into the noise-normalized evaluation.
  • Synthetic ABRs come from a restricted waveform family; EEG noise is white and its simulated level is known. Real EEG may behave differently. The interim and guidance test results have been inspected, so a fresh holdout is needed for a final comparison after further tuning.

Future work can compare training-set sizes while holding the training budget and evaluation protocol fixed, and include realistic waveforms and correlated noise.
